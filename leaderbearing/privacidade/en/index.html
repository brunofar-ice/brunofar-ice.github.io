Privacy Policy — Leader Bearing

Versão em português

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy describes how the Leader Bearing app (com.bruno.leadernotes) handles data. Google Play requires it to be at a public URL, outside the app. The address goes in the Console and on the store listing.

Who we are

Leader Bearing is developed by Bruno Guedes Faria. For any privacy matter, including requests for access or deletion, write to bgfsoft@gmail.com.

Summary

Leader Bearing works offline and without an account. Everything you record about your team stays only on your device. We have no server and we receive no data from you or your team. Purchases are made through Google Play.

1. What stays only on your device

This data stays in the app's private storage and is never sent by us:

  • Team data that you record: names, job titles, profile fields, notes, 1-on-1 sessions, feedback, PDI goals, metrics, trainings, reminders, and profile photos.
  • Preferences: language, theme, filters, shortcuts, and lock settings.

Android's automatic cloud backup and the data transfer when you switch phones are turned off for this app: this data does not go to Google Drive or to another phone unless you choose to send it (through the app's own backup). Uninstalling the app erases everything.

You are responsible for your team's data. The app stores information about other people (your direct reports). Use it in line with the data protection law of your country (in Brazil, the LGPD, Brazil's data protection law) and with your company's rules.

2. Device permissions

  • Contacts (optional): used only when you ask for a team member's photo from the contacts or tap "Update All Photos from Contacts". Before the Android request, the app explains the use. The contacts are read on the device. The app stores only the photo you chose or that it found. No contact is copied or sent.
  • Notifications (optional): to alert you about the reminders that you create. They are local notifications, scheduled on the device itself, with no server.
  • Biometrics or screen lock (optional): if you turn on the lock, Android checks your fingerprint, face, or PIN. The app does not receive or store biometric data. It only receives the answer "allowed" or "denied" from the system.
  • Google Play Billing: to buy and restore the Pro plan.

The app also blocks screenshots and hides its content in the recent apps list.

3. Backups and files that you export

Backups (JSON, encrypted with a password or in plain text) and CSV spreadsheets are created only when you ask. The app opens the Android share sheet and you choose the destination (Drive, e-mail, another app). From then on, the file follows the rules of the destination you chose. The encrypted backup uses AES-256-GCM with a password that only you know. Without it, no one can open it, not even us.

The texts that you share (1-on-1 agenda, summary, PDI plan, dossier) also leave the app only when you choose the destination.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play. We do not see or store payment data. The app receives only the purchase confirmation from Play, checks its digital signature on the device itself, and unlocks Pro. To cancel, use "Manage or cancel in Google Play" in the app or the subscriptions area of Play.

Google's billing library, included in the app, may send Google diagnostic data about its operation. This processing is done by Google, as controller: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. What we do not do

  • We have no server and we receive no data.
  • We do not use ads, advertising identifiers, or tracking.
  • We do not use any usage analytics tool or third-party crash reporting.
  • We do not sell or share data.

6. Children

The app is a work tool for adult managers. It is not directed at anyone under 18 years of age.

7. Your rights (LGPD and GDPR)

Because the data stays only on your device, you control it yourself:

  • View and correct: everything is on the app's screens.
  • Export: Settings > "Backups & AES-256 Vault" or "CSV Spreadsheet".
  • Erase everything: Settings > "GDPR Privacy & Data Purge" > "Permanently Erase All Data", or uninstall the app.
  • Withdraw a permission: Android Settings > Apps > Leader Bearing > Permissions.
  • Ask a question: through the contact e-mail above.

Purchase data and Google account data are held by Google. Requests about them go through Google's channels.

8. Security

There is no database of ours to be breached. The data stays in the app's private storage, isolated by Android. You can turn on the lock by biometrics or PIN, with automatic lock after inactivity.

9. Changes to this policy

If data handling changes (for example, cloud sync), this policy will be updated before the change reaches the app, and the date at the top changes with it.
